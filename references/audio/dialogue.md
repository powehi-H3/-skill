# Dialogue and Audio

Separate three domains:

1. Dialogue / vocal delivery
2. Diegetic soundscape
3. Non-diegetic music

## Exact text

User-provided dialogue, lyrics, and visible text should remain in the original language and wording unless the user requests a change.

## Voice reference

A voice reference may specify:

- timbre
- delivery
- accent
- pacing

Do not copy semantic dialogue from a voice reference unless explicitly requested.

## Cross-shot continuity

Keep the same speaker identity stable across shots. If a line continues across a cut, preserve continuity rather than accidentally restarting or duplicating it.
