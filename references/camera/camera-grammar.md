# Camera Grammar

Camera language should be concrete and local to the shot.

Useful dimensions:

- viewpoint / POV
- shot size
- camera position
- camera orientation
- camera movement
- framing
- subject visibility

Do not stack incompatible movements.

Do not add a camera choice merely because a prompt would look more cinematic with one.

If the user gives "POV", preserve POV as the camera constraint rather than translating it into a generic eye-level shot.
